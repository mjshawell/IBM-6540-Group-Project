Project Overview

Understanding how discount strategy influences retail performance

This project applies machine learning and statistical modeling to examine how discounts, marketing spend, customer characteristics, and promotional strategy influence sales performance. Our analysis focuses on identifying relationships that retailers can use to improve pricing and marketing decisions.

Analysis Framework

Research Objectives

Each group member investigated a different component of the relationship between discounting, marketing activity, customer behavior, and retail performance.

RO1

Discount Strategy

Analyze how discount levels influence retail sales and revenue performance.

RO2

Sales & Customer Behavior

Evaluate patterns in customer purchasing behavior and their relationship with promotional activity.

RO3

Predictive Performance

Apply machine learning techniques to identify important predictors of retail sales outcomes.

RO4

Promotional Effectiveness

Examine additional promotional and retail factors associated with stronger sales performance.

RO5

Marketing Spend × Discount Interaction

Analyze whether the relationship between discounts and sales changes depending on the level of marketing investment.
